Contains a regional spatial layer in which individual tracts are dissolved into larger protected-area units, such as entire national wildlife refuges. Individual tract boundaries and tract-level attributes are not retained in this version. This format provides a consolidated representation of protected areas across the Lower Rio Grande Valley, similar in concept to the World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA), but intended to provide comprehensive coverage of protected areas throughout the region. It is suitable for regional mapping and analyses that do not require individual tract boundaries.
